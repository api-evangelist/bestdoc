# BestDoc

> BestDoc is a healthcare patient-relationship-management (PRM) company that helps
> hospitals improve the patient experience across inpatient and outpatient care.
> Its products span concierge/inpatient experience management, outpatient practice
> management, omni-channel patient feedback (BestDoc Survey), an AI Reply Assistant
> for review responses, discharge management, and continued-care engagement. BestDoc
> Technology Pvt. Ltd. is an ISO 27001:2013 certified company operating from Kochi,
> Bangalore, and Dubai. This is an API Evangelist network profile; BestDoc does not
> publish a public developer API, OpenAPI specification, or SDKs at this time.
